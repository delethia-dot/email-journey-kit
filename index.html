The goal is not to send more email. The goal is to create a path from interest to customer.

Batch-and-blast treats every subscriber as the same person on the same day. It is easy to run and it teaches you nothing, because the response tells you as much about your timing as it does about their intent.

A journey works differently. You capture something useful at the gate, watch what people do with what you send, and let that behavior decide the next message. The kit gives you the structure for doing that on whatever platform you already run.

The safety rails

Automation runs the ride. You still design it.
